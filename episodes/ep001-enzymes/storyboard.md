# EP001 — Storyboard

Style frames for the key moments: `storyboard/storyboard-sheet.jpg` (individual frames are in the same folder).
Each beat is tied to a `{cue}` in `script.md`; when the narration is generated, every cue becomes the exact
frame the word is spoken, and the motion below starts on that frame.

**Visual language:** deep ink-blue world; teal = amylase (our enzyme); cream hexagons = glucose locked in starch;
**amber glow = sweet** (the motif that pays off the hook); coral = heat / myths; violet = other enzymes.
Molecules always jiggle a little (thermal motion) and jiggle *more* when it's hotter.

**Sound:** a soft ambient music bed, ducked under the voice. SFX only on meaningful events:
soft "pop" when a substrate binds, crisp "snip" when a bond breaks, low "whoosh" on the zoom, gentle ticks
during the pause-and-predict countdown, a warm rising tone when the cracker turns sweet.

### S09 · Pause and predict (~15 s)
Thermometer ticks to 50, 60, 70 on each spoken number (coral heat shimmer). Two dashed guesses appear
past the peak (up? down?). Then the **Pause & predict** overlay: scene dims, pause glyph, a 4-second
countdown ring with soft ticks. Question: "Keep heating: faster, or slower?"

| Cue | What we see |
|---|---|

| `{cold}` `{rewarm}` | A healthy enzyme at 5 °C: barely moving, shape intact. Warm to 37 °C: it speeds up and snips again. |

### S11 · Myth #2: heat kills enzymes (~20 s)
Myth card: "Heat ~~kills~~ **denatures** enzymes". The enzyme labelled "molecule" (not alive). Then side by side:
healthy enzyme with substrate fitting ✓ vs denatured enzyme with substrate not fitting — label
"active site changes shape" (the exam answer).

### S12 · pH (~27 s)
pH bar (coral acid → white neutral → violet alkaline). An enzyme slid towards either end visibly warps.
Graph: two bell curves draw on — **pepsin** (violet) peaking at pH 2, labelled "stomach";
**amylase** (teal) peaking at pH 7, labelled "mouth".

### S13 · Resolve (~30 s)
Back to the cracker crumbs from the hook, zoom in (reverse of S03): many amylase molecules snipping starch
into glowing maltose. Mini thermometer at 37 °C ✓ and pH 7 ✓. The hourglass/stopwatch card from the hook
returns: "millions of years" vs "while you chew". Pull out: the crumbs glow amber, sweetness meter full.
Title returns.

### S14 · End (4 s)
Recap row of three icons (pocket shape · thermometer · pH) and the channel name. Music resolves.
